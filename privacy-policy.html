When you use our enquiry form, we may collect the following information:

  • Full Name

We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information through the website contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or request.
  • Contact you regarding the services you have asked about.
  • Provide information about our accounting, taxation, GST, ROC and other professional services.
  • Communicate with you through WhatsApp, telephone or email.
  • Maintain appropriate records of enquiries and client communications.

Our website provides WhatsApp contact buttons and an enquiry form. When you submit the enquiry form, the information entered by you is used to prepare a WhatsApp message addressed to our business WhatsApp number.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as Google Maps, Google Fonts, WhatsApp and external services used to provide website functionality. These services may process information according to their own policies.

7. Cookies

Our website does not intentionally use cookies to collect personal information for advertising or profiling purposes. Third-party services used by the website may have their own technical mechanisms or policies.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us if you wish to ask about the personal information that we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where applicable and legally permissible.

You may contact us using the details provided below.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for maintaining business records, or where retention is required by applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general business and professional services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website for independent use of our services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy
